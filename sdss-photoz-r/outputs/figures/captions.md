# Figure captions (drafts)
- **F01:** Spectroscopic redshifts of the 100 SDSS galaxies: histogram, kernel density and rug. Range 0.025–0.221, median 0.101.
- **F02:** Distributions of de-reddened u, g, r, i, z magnitudes for the 100 galaxies. Galaxies are fainter in u (larger magnitude) and brighter towards the red. The dashed line marks the Main Galaxy Sample limit r = 17.77 used in the query.
- **F03:** u − g versus g − r for the 100 galaxies, coloured by spectroscopic redshift. Higher-redshift galaxies sit at redder g − r.
- **F04:** Colours of 100 SDSS galaxies versus spectroscopic redshift. Solid lines are LOESS fits with 95% bands; r is the Pearson correlation. g − r has the strongest correlation (r = 0.54): it reddens as the 4000 Å break moves through the g band.
- **F05:** Pearson correlations among the five features and z_spec. The feature most correlated with redshift is g − r (r = 0.54).
- **F06:** r-band magnitude versus spectroscopic redshift with a LOESS fit. More distant galaxies are fainter on average (inverse-square law); the dashed line is the r = 17.77 flux limit of the sample. Pearson r = 0.40.
- **F20:** Observed wavelength of the 4000 Å break, 4000(1 + z) Å (black line), over the SDSS filter bands (shaded: effective wavelength ± FWHM/2, approximate). The grey band marks this sample (z = 0.025–0.221), where the break lies at 4098–4885 Å, inside the g band. It would leave g only at z ≈ 0.34.
